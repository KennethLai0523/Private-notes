2.Attribute 
functional - whether the software does the thing it suppose to do 
e.g. completeness , correctness , appropriate 
non function - how it does it (everything else)
e.g.realibility , availability , portability (devices type) , testability , scalability, flexbility, reusability, maintainability, Interoperability, performance , security

3.type of testing 
01 - unit testing (part of a code)
02 - integration testing (part of the code works but not together)
03 - system testing (entire piece of software)
04 - acceptance testing (from a user perspective, meet the software requirement)
